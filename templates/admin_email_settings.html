Resend Setup Guide
  1. Create a free account at resend.com (3,000 emails/month free, no credit card required).
  2. Go to Domains and add + verify your sending domain by adding the provided DNS records at your registrar.
  3. Go to API Keys, create a new key, and copy the re_... value.
  4. In your Render / Railway dashboard, add these environment variables:
    RESEND_API_KEY      = re_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
    RESEND_SENDER_EMAIL = hello@yourdomain.com
    RESEND_SENDER_NAME  = HealthBridge NGO
  5. Redeploy your service and click Send Test Email above to confirm delivery.
