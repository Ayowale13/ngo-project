Who We Are

HealthBridge was founded in 2016 by a group of medical professionals and community advocates who saw a critical gap in healthcare access for underserved populations across Nigeria.

What began as weekend clinic outreaches in two communities has grown into a nationally recognized health NGO operating across 12 states, serving over 12,000 individuals annually.

"We believe healthcare is not a privilege — it is a fundamental right every human being deserves."
